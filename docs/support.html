Common Questions

I can't sign up or sign in

Double-check that your email is entered correctly and your password is at least 6 characters. If the app says "email already in use," you may have signed up previously — try resetting the password. If the sign-in screen never responds, close and reopen the app; a network hiccup during boot can leave the auth handshake stuck.

The AI Judge failed to score my build

The AI reviews photos of finished kits. If it declines a submission, try one of the following:

  • Take the photo in normal light against a plain background — no glare, no busy backdrops.
  • Make sure the entire kit is in frame and the shot is in focus.
  • Only one build per photo. The AI can't grade multiple kits in one shot.
  • Photos that don't contain a mecha model kit will be declined by design.

If the Judge times out repeatedly with a clean photo, that's a server issue on our end — email support with the approximate time you tried and we'll investigate.

Subscription, billing, or free trial

Hanger's subscription is processed by Google Play (or Apple, on iOS). All billing questions — including refunds, trial cancellations, and payment method issues — are handled by the store directly.

  • Cancel or manage: Google Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hanger.
  • Refund request: Google Play's refund page at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory. Refunds within 48 hours of purchase are typically automatic.
  • Restore a purchase on a new device: open Hanger → Settings → Restore purchases. Sign in with the same Google account that made the original purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Open Hanger → Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, all kits in your hangar, all posted builds, comments, likes, and follows. It cannot be undone. Any active subscription is not automatically cancelled — cancel it through the store separately, or you'll continue to be billed.

If you can't sign in to reach the delete option, email support from the address on the account and we'll delete it manually within 5 business days.

Report or block another pilot

Open the pilot's profile → tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right → choose Report pilot or Block pilot. You can also report an individual comment by long-pressing it. Reports go straight to us for review; blocks hide the pilot's content from your feed instantly and unfollow them.

Push notifications aren't showing up

Check the following in order:

  1. Hanger's Settings → Push notifications is on.
  2. Your device's Settings → Apps → Hanger → Notifications is on.
  3. You're not in Do Not Disturb / Focus mode.
  4. Sign out and back in — that re-registers your device token.

Feature requests or general feedback

We love hearing what would make Hanger better for how you build. Email support with "Feature request" in the subject and a short description of what you're trying to do. Not every idea ships, but every idea gets read.

Bug reports

When emailing about a bug, please include:

  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • What you expected vs. what actually happened.
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g., Pixel 8, Android 15).
  • Hanger version (Settings → About).
  • Screenshots or a screen recording if you have them.

Privacy and data
